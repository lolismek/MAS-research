Boucher)\n←\n1976\nSeasons\n1978\n→\nThe 1977\nNew York Yankees\nseason was the 75th season for the Yankees. The team finished with a record of 100–62 and won the\nWorld Series\n. The series victory was the 21st title in franchise history and the first under the ownership of\nGeorge Steinbrenner\n. New York was managed by\nBilly Martin\nand played at\nYankee Stadium\nin\nThe Bronx\n. The season was brought to life years later in the book and drama-documentary\nThe Bronx is Burning\n.\nThe Yankees also hosted the\n1977 Major League Baseball All-Star Game\n. To date, this is the most recent time the All-Star Game host team has won the World Series the same year.\nOffseason\n[\nedit\n]\nThe Yankees signed\nReggie Jackson\nto a five-year contract, totaling\n$\n2.96 million, on November 29, 1976. Upon arriving in New York, Jackson asked for uniform number 9, which he had worn in\nOakland\nand\nBaltimore\n. However, that number was being worn by\nthird baseman\nGraig Nettles\n. So, noting that then-all-time home run leader\nHank Aaron\nhad just retired, Jackson asked for and received number 44, Aaron's number.\nNotable transactions\n[\nedit\n]\nNovember 5, 1976:\nJim Mason\nwas drafted from the Yankees by the\nToronto Blue Jays\nas the 30th pick in the\n1976 MLB expansion draft\n.\n[\n1\n]\nJanuary 11, 1977:\nWillie McGee\nwas drafted by the Yankees in the first round (15th pick) of the\n1977 Major League Baseball draft\n(secondary phase) and he signed on June 6.\n[\n2\n]\nJanuary 20, 1977:\nElliott Maddox\nand\nRick Bladt\nwere traded by the Yankees to the\nBaltimore Orioles\nfor\nPaul Blair\n.\n[\n3\n]\nFebruary 17, 1977:\nSandy Alomar Sr.\nwas traded by the Yankees to the\nTexas Rangers\nfor\nBrian Doyle\n,\nGreg Pryor\nand cash.\n[\n4\n]\nMarch 26, 1977:\nKerry Dineen\nwas traded by the Yankees to the\nPhiladelphia Phillies\nfor\nSergio Ferrer\n.\n[\n5\n]\nRegular season\n[\nedit\n]\nThe team finished in first place in the\nAmerican League East\nwith a record of 100–62 (.617),  2½ games ahead of the\nBaltimore Orioles\nto successfully defend their division title.
