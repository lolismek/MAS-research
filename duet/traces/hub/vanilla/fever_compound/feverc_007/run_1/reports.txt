===== edge 1 =====
I investigated Babur's historical role and title to determine if he was merely a soldier or a ruler.

**Findings:**
Babur (Zahir-ud-din Muhammad) was a sovereign ruler and the founder of the Mughal Empire. He held the title of Mughal Emperor (Padishah) from 1526 until his death in 1530. Prior to that, he was the Emir of Kabul, ruling from 1504 to 1526. He was a descendant of both Timur and Genghis Khan.

**Key Evidence:**
*   **Conquest of India:** Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi at the First Battle of Panipat in 1526, establishing the Mughal Empire in Northern India.
*   **Territorial Control:** He successfully claimed and ruled over significant lands, including Kabul, the Punjab, Delhi, and Agra.
*   **Titles:** Historical records consistently refer to him as an Emperor and Emir, not just a soldier. He was also a noted poet and diarist (author of the Baburnama).

**Conclusion:**
The claim that Babur was "only a soldier and was unable to claim any land for his own" is **REFUTES**. He was a successful conqueror and the first Mughal Emperor who ruled over a vast empire.
