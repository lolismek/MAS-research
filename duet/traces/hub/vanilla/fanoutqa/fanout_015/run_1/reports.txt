===== edge 1 =====
I investigated the 1st and 2nd most widely spoken languages in the world as of late November 2023.

**Answer:**
1. English
2. Mandarin Chinese

**Findings and Evidence:**
Based on data from Ethnologue (2023/2024 editions) and the CIA World Factbook (2022/2023 data), the ranking is determined by the total number of speakers (native + non-native).

*   **English** is ranked 1st with approximately 1.45 to 1.5 billion total speakers. It has roughly 380 million native speakers but over 1 billion second-language speakers, making it the most widely spoken language globally.
*   **Mandarin Chinese** is ranked 2nd with approximately 1.1 to 1.14 billion total speakers. While it has the highest number of native speakers (approx. 900-940 million), its total speaker count is lower than English's due to fewer second-language speakers.

This consensus is supported by major sources including Britannica, Statista, and the U.S. State Department.

Findings:
I researched the rankings of languages by total number of speakers (native and non-native) using data from Ethnologue and other linguistic sources available in late 2023.

The top 5 languages by total speakers are:
1. English (~1.5 billion)
2. Mandarin Chinese (~1.1 billion)
3. Hindi (~609 million)
4. Spanish (~559 million)
5. French (~310 million)

Answer:
The 4th most widely spoken language is Spanish.

Ethnologue data (2023/2024 editions) consistently ranks Spanish as the fourth most spoken language globally when counting both native and second-language speakers, with approximately 559 million speakers. This ranking places it behind English, Mandarin Chinese, and Hindi.
