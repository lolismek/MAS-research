**Findings:**
*   **Canada:** The highest mountain is **Mount Logan**, located in the Yukon territory. It stands at 5,959 meters (19,551 feet).
*   **Indonesia:** The highest mountain is **Puncak Jaya** (also known as Carstensz Pyramid), located on the island of New Guinea. It stands at 4,884 meters (16,024 feet).

**Key Evidence:**
Search results and geographical records confirm Puncak Jaya as the highest peak in Indonesia and the highest point on the island of New Guinea. For Canada, Mount Logan is consistently cited as the highest peak in the country and the second-highest in North America.

**Answer:**
Mount Logan, Puncak Jaya

===== edge 3 =====
I investigated the highest mountain in Russia. Based on multiple authoritative sources including Wikipedia, Britannica, and NASA, the highest point in Russia is Mount Elbrus. It is a dormant stratovolcano located in the Caucasus Mountains with an elevation of 5,642 meters (18,510 feet). It is also widely recognized as the highest mountain in Europe.

Answer: Mount Elbrus
