Assignment Report: #1 Most-Visited Website and Owner (November 2023)

**Assignment:** Identify the #1 most-visited website globally as of November 2023 and determine its owner.

**Findings:**
Based on global web traffic data for late 2023, the #1 most-visited website is google.com.

**Answer Reached:**
The #1 most-visited website is google.com. It is owned by Google LLC, which is a wholly owned subsidiary of the holding company Alphabet Inc.

**Key Evidence:**
- **Traffic Rankings:** Data from major analytics firms (e.g., SimilarWeb, Semrush) consistently rank google.com as the most visited website globally throughout 2023, significantly ahead of competitors like YouTube and Facebook.
- **Corporate Structure:** Google LLC is the legal entity that operates the search engine and associated services. Since the corporate restructuring in 2015, Google LLC has been a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc., which holds the parent ownership.

Report on Assignment: #2 Most-Visited Website and Owner (November 2023)

Assignment: Identify the #2 most-visited website globally as of November 2023 and determine its owner.

Findings:
The #2 most-visited website globally as of November 2023 is YouTube (youtube.com).
The owner of YouTube is Google LLC, which operates as a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc.

Evidence:
1. Traffic Rankings: Global web traffic data from sources such as SimilarWeb and Semrush for late 2023 consistently rank Google.com as the #1 website and YouTube.com as the #2 website worldwide.
2. Ownership History: Google acquired YouTube in November 2006 for $1.65 billion. Since the acquisition, YouTube has operated as a subsidiary of Google, which is part of the Alphabet Inc. holding company structure established in 2015.
