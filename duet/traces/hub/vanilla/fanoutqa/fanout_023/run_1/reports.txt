Findings:
I determined that **Merlot** is the most planted grape variety in the Bordeaux appellation, accounting for approximately 60-66% of all vineyard plantings. Key Evidence:
- **Planting Statistics:** Multiple sources (including Wikipedia and Decanter) confirm Merlot is the dominant variety in the region, significantly outnumbering Cabernet Sauvignon.

This is attributed to the Right Bank's clay and limestone soils, which favor Merlot over Cabernet Sauvignon.

Answer Reached:
Most planted variety: Merlot
Most planted area: Right Bank

**Findings:**
The second most planted grape variety in the Bordeaux appellation is **Cabernet Sauvignon**. It accounts for approximately 22% to 25% of the region's red grape plantings, ranking behind Merlot (which is the most planted at roughly 62-66%) and ahead of Cabernet Franc (approx. 9-12%).

Cabernet Sauvignon is most planted in the **Médoc** (often referred to broadly as the **Left Bank**). This area, located north of the Gironde estuary, is the primary stronghold for the variety, where it dominates the blends in prestigious appellations such as Pauillac, Margaux, and Saint-Estèphe.

**Key Evidence:**
*   **Statistics:** Data from the official Vins de Bordeaux website and wine industry sources (e.g., Wine Searcher, The Wine Cellar Insider) consistently list Merlot as #1 and Cabernet Sauvignon as #2 in terms of planted hectares.
*   **Geography:** Wine encyclopedias and regional guides explicitly state that Cabernet Sauvignon "dominates the blend in red wines produced in the Médoc and the rest of the left bank."

**Answer:** Cabernet Sauvignon, Médoc
