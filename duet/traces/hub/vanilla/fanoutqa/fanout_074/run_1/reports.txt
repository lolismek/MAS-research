Assignment: Identify the widely recognized inventors of the internet.

Findings:
The internet was not the result of a single invention but a collaborative evolution involving many pioneers. However, the most widely recognized inventors are **Vinton Cerf** and **Bob Kahn**.

Key Evidence:
- **TCP/IP Protocols:** Cerf and Kahn developed the Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP) in 1974. This set of rules governs how data is transmitted across networks and serves as the technical foundation of the modern internet.
- **"Fathers of the Internet":** They are frequently cited with this title in historical accounts and encyclopedias (e.g., Britannica, BBC) due to their pivotal role in enabling different computer networks to communicate with each other, effectively creating a "network of networks."
- **Distinction from the Web:** While Tim Berners-Lee is often mentioned in this context, he is specifically credited with inventing the World Wide Web (the system of interlinked hypertext documents), not the underlying internet infrastructure itself.

Answer: Vinton Cerf and Bob Kahn
