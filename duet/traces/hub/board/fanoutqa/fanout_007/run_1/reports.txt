===== edge 3 =====
I investigated the sign language associated with Spanish.

**Findings:**
The sign language used in Spain is officially known as **Spanish Sign Language** (Spanish: *Lengua de Signos Española*, abbreviated as **LSE**). It was officially recognized by the Spanish government in 2007.

**Important Distinction:**
It is crucial to note that "Spanish Sign Language" (LSE) is specific to Spain. Spanish-speaking countries outside of Spain have their own distinct sign languages (e.g., Mexican Sign Language/LSM in Mexico, Argentine Sign Language/LSA in Argentina). However, the direct answer to "the sign language for Spanish" (referring to the language of Spain) is LSE.

**Answer:**
Spanish Sign Language (Lengua de Signos Española, LSE)
