===== edge 2 =====
Assignment: Determine the founding year of the second most popular newspaper in the US as of late November 2023.

Findings: Based on 2023 circulation and subscriber data, the top 5 most popular US newspapers are ranked as follows: 1) The New York Times, 2) The Wall Street Journal, 3) The Washington Post, 4) Los Angeles Times, 5) The Boston Globe. The second newspaper on this list is The Wall Street Journal.

Answer: 1889

Key Evidence: Multiple authoritative sources (Wikipedia, Britannica, and The Wall Street Journal's own "About Us" page) confirm that The Wall Street Journal was founded and published its first issue on July 8, 1889, by Charles Dow, Edward Jones, and Charles Bergstresser.
