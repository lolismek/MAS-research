I investigated the recognized inventors of the airplane. The answer is Orville Wright and Wilbur Wright (the Wright brothers). The key evidence is their achievement of the first controlled, sustained, powered flight on December 17, 1903, at Kitty Hawk, North Carolina, which is historically credited as the birth of aviation.

I investigated the recognized inventors of the internet. The answer is Vinton Cerf and Robert Kahn. They are widely known as the "fathers of the Internet" for designing the TCP/IP protocols, which established the fundamental standards for data transmission that allow the internet to function.

He is widely recognized as the "father of the personal computer" for designing and building the **Altair 8800** in 1974. The Altair 8800 is considered the first commercially successful personal computer and is credited with kickstarting the microcomputer revolution. While other figures like Steve Wozniak (Apple II) and Alan Kay (Dynabook) made significant contributions to the evolution of personal computing, Ed Roberts is the most distinct answer for the invention of the personal computer category itself.

Assignment: Identify the recognized inventors of the mobile phone and the digital camera.

Findings:
- Mobile Phone: Martin Cooper is widely recognized as the inventor of the handheld mobile phone. He led the engineering team at Motorola that developed the first portable cellular phone and made the first public handheld call on April 3, 1973.
- Digital Camera: Steven Sasson is recognized as the inventor of the digital camera. He developed the first self-contained, portable digital camera at Eastman Kodak in 1975, using a CCD sensor and cassette tape for storage.

Answer: Martin Cooper, Steven Sasson

Key Evidence: Historical records and industry recognition confirm Cooper's 1973 DynaTAC prototype demonstration, earning him the title "father of the cell phone." Sasson's 1975 Kodak invention is documented in engineering archives as the first functional, self-contained digital camera, establishing his primary inventor status.
