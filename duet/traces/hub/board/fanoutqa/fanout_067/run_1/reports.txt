As of November 20, 2023, the five richest people in the world were Bernard Arnault & family, Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos, Larry Ellison, and Warren Buffett. Here are their educational backgrounds:

1. Bernard Arnault: Graduated from HEC Paris (1971) and École Polytechnique (1973). Attended Lycée Henri-IV for high school.
2. Elon Musk: Graduated from the University of Pennsylvania (Wharton School of Business and College of Arts & Sciences). Attended Pretoria Boys High School.
3. Jeff Bezos: Graduated from Princeton University. Attended Miami Palmetto Senior High School.
4. Larry Ellison: Did not earn a university degree (attended University of Chicago and University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign). Graduated from Glenbrook South High School.
5. Warren Buffett: Graduated from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and Columbia Business School. Graduated from Woodrow Wilson High School in Washington, D.C.

All rankings were verified against the Forbes Real-Time Billionaires list for November 2023. Educational details are based on standard biographical records.
