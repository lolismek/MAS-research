Assignment: Did Benjamin Franklin serve as a spokesman or representative for the American colonies in Great Britain?

Findings:
Benjamin Franklin served as a colonial agent in London for several colonies, including Pennsylvania, Georgia, New Jersey, and Massachusetts. He was the leading spokesman for American interests in England, particularly during the Stamp Act crisis in 1766, where he gave highly publicized testimony before the House of Commons.

Conclusion:
The evidence confirms that Benjamin Franklin served as a spokesman and representative for the American colonies in Great Britain. Therefore, the claim is SUPPORTED.
