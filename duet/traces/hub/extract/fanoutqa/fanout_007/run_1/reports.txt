**Findings:**
The primary sign language used by the Deaf community in mainland China, where Mandarin is the dominant spoken language, is **Chinese Sign Language** (CSL). In Chinese, it is known as **Zhongguo Shouyu** (ZGS) or 中国手语.

While there are regional variations such as Taiwan Sign Language (TSL) in Taiwan, CSL is the standard natural sign language corresponding to the Mandarin-speaking population in the People's Republic of China. There is also a manually coded system called "Signed Chinese" (Wenfa Shouyu), but CSL is the natural language of the community.

**Answer:**
Chinese Sign Language (CSL)
