**Findings:**
Babur was not merely a soldier; he was a multifaceted historical figure. His primary roles and professions included:
1.  **Ruler/Emperor:** He was the founder of the Mughal Empire and ruled over Fergana, Kabul, and parts of India.
2.  **Poet and Writer:** He was a skilled poet and the author of the *Baburnama*, a famous autobiography and diary.
3.  **Statesman and Garden-builder:** He is credited with introducing the charbagh (four-part garden) style to India.

**Assessment of the Claim:**
The claim asserts that Babur was "only a soldier" and "unable to claim any land."
-   **Refutation of "only a soldier":** Evidence shows he was a poet, diarist, and statesman.
-   **Refutation of "unable to claim land":** Babur successfully claimed and ruled significant territories, including his ancestral home of Fergana, the city of Kabul, and the vast territories of the Mughal Empire in India.

**Conclusion:**
The claim is **REFUTES**.
