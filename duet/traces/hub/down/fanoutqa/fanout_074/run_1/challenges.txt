===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: The report only covers the airplane; could you please provide the inventors for the internet, personal computer, mobile phone, and digital camera as well?
ANSWER: The inventors of the airplane are Orville and Wilbur Wright. The internet is credited to Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn for their development of the TCP/IP protocols. The personal computer is attributed to a collective effort, with key figures including Ed Roberts (creator of the Altair 8800), Steve Wozniak (co-creator of the Apple I), and Alan Kay (conceptualizer of the Dynabook). The mobile phone was invented by Martin Cooper. The digital camera was invented by Steven Sasson.
