===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: The report only provides university information for Heng Swee Keat; please provide the universities attended by all five Deputy Prime Ministers listed.
ANSWER: Wong Kan Seng attended the National University of Singapore (NUS). Teo Chee Hean also attended the National University of Singapore (NUS). Tharman Shanmugaratnam attended the National University of Singapore (NUS) and the University of Oxford. Heng Swee Keat attended the University of Cambridge and Harvard University. Lawrence Wong attended the National University of Singapore (NUS).
