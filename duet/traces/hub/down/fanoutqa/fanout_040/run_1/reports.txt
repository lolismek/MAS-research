Key Findings:
- Name: Galdhøpiggen
- Elevation: 2,469 meters (8,100 feet)
- Location: Lom Municipality, Innlandet county, Norway
- Significance: It is the highest peak in Norway, Scandinavia, and Northern Europe.

Evidence:
Search results from Wikipedia, Visit Norway, and other geographical sources consistently identify Galdhøpiggen as the highest point. While there was a historical dispute with Glittertinden regarding glacier height, Galdhøpiggen is currently accepted as the highest peak.
