QUESTION: What specific 2023 circulation or subscriber data supports ranking the Boston Globe and Los Angeles Times above USA Today and the New York Post in total popularity?
ANSWER: Based on the 2023 data observed, the ranking is supported by total subscriber counts (combining print and digital). The Los Angeles Times had a total of approximately 306,500 subscribers (243,000 digital + 63,500 print), and the Boston Globe had approximately 306,100 subscribers (260,500 digital + 45,600 print). In contrast, USA Today had a total of roughly 231,412 subscribers (142,212 digital + 89,200 print). Although USA Today has higher print circulation, the Globe and LA Times have significantly larger digital subscriber bases, resulting in a higher total reach. The New York Post, while having high print circulation (around 131,000), has a much smaller digital presence, placing its total popularity below the Globe and LA Times.
