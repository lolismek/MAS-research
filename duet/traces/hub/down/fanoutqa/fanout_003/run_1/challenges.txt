QUESTION: Can you provide the names and birth dates of the other confirmed nominees (Lai Ching-te, Hou Yu-ih, Ko Wen-je) and clarify Wang Jin-pyng's official candidacy status as of November 20, 2023?
ANSWER: As of November 20, 2023, the confirmed nominees were DPP's Lai Ching-te (born August 6, 1959), KMT's Hou Yu-ih (born October 13, 1961), and TPP's Ko Wen-je (born October 21, 1959). Although he had been widely speculated to potentially run as an independent, he officially announced around that time that he would not enter the presidential race.
