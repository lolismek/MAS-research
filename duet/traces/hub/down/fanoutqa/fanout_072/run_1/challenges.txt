===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: The report only covers the Texas Rangers; can you please provide the General Managers for the Houston Astros, Atlanta Braves, and Los Angeles Dodgers as of November 20, 2023?
ANSWER: As of November 20, 2023, the General Managers (or top baseball operations executives) for the four most recent World Series champions were Chris Young for the Texas Rangers, Dana Brown for the Houston Astros, Alex Anthopoulos for the Atlanta Braves, and Andrew Friedman (who served as President of Baseball Operations, fulfilling the GM role) for the Los Angeles Dodgers.

===== ask 2 =====
QUESTION: This report only covers the Houston Astros, but the task asks for the GMs of the four most recent World Series winners (Rangers, Diamondbacks, Braves, Dodgers); can you provide the GMs for those four teams as of November 20, 2023?
ANSWER: As of November 20, 2023, the General Managers for the four most recent World Series winners were Chris Young for the Texas Rangers (2023 champions), Mike Hazen for the Arizona Diamondbacks (2022 champions), Alex Anthopoulos for the Atlanta Braves (2021 champions), and Andrew Friedman for the Los Angeles Dodgers (2020 champions).
