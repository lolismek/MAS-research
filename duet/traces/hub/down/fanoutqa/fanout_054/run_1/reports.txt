===== edge 1 =====
I have completed the investigation into the top 5 most populous cities in the world as of late November 2023.

**Findings:**
Based on the United Nations World Urbanization Prospects (2018 Revision) and consistent data from demographic sources like CityPopulation.de, the rankings for urban agglomerations in 2023 are as follows:

1.  **Tokyo**, Japan (approx. 37.4 million)
2.  **Delhi**, India (approx. 32.9 million)
3.  **Shanghai**, China (approx. 28.5 million)
4.  **Dhaka**, Bangladesh (approx. 23.2 million)
5.  **São Paulo**, Brazil (approx. 22.5 million)

**Key Evidence:**
*   **Metric:** The ranking uses "urban agglomeration" population, which is the standard international metric for comparing city sizes, as it accounts for the continuous built-up area rather than just administrative boundaries (which can artificially inflate numbers for cities like Chongqing).
*   **Source:** The UN World Urbanization Prospects 2018 revision is the primary dataset used for 2023 projections. While some newer estimates suggest Delhi may be approaching or surpassing Tokyo, Tokyo remains the standard #1 in the 2023 data cycle.

**Answer:**
Tokyo, Delhi, Shanghai, Dhaka, São Paulo
