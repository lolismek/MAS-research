===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: Your report only covers two varieties, but the task requires five; could you please provide the remaining three most popular grape varieties and their primary planting areas in Bordeaux?
ANSWER: Based on the standard Bordeaux grape hierarchy, the next three most popular varieties after Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon are Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot, and Malbec. Cabernet Franc is the third most planted red variety and is primarily grown on the **Right Bank** (Saint-Émilion and Pomerol), where it thrives in clay and limestone soils. Petit Verdot and Malbec are minor components of the traditional blend; they are most commonly found in the **Left Bank** (Médoc and Graves) and the **Entre-Deux-Mers** region, where they are used to add color and structure to the blends, though their plantings have significantly decreased in recent decades.

FINAL ANSWER: Cabernet Franc, Right Bank, Petit Verdot, Left Bank, Malbec, Left Bank

===== ask 2 =====
QUESTION: What are the primary planting areas within Bordeaux for the top five grape varieties listed (Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc, Sémillon, and Sauvignon Blanc)?
ANSWER: Merlot is the most widely planted variety, dominating the Right Bank (particularly Saint-Émilion and Pomerol) and also extensively grown on the Left Bank (Médoc and Graves). Cabernet Sauvignon is the primary grape of the Left Bank, thriving in the gravelly soils of the Médoc and Graves. Cabernet Franc is most prominent on the Right Bank (Saint-Émilion and Pomerol) and also plays a significant role in Graves. For white wines, Sémillon is the dominant variety in the sweet wine appellations of Sauternes and Barsac, as well as in Graves for dry whites. Sauvignon Blanc is widely planted across the region for dry white wines, with significant presence in Graves, Entre-Deux-Mers, and Bordeaux Supérieur.

===== ask 3 =====
QUESTION: You listed the five varieties but only provided the planting area for the fifth one; could you please specify which area of Bordeaux the other four varieties (Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Franc, and Malbec) are most planted in?
ANSWER: Merlot is most planted in the **Right Bank** (specifically Saint-Émilion and Pomerol), while Cabernet Sauvignon is most planted in the **Left Bank** (specifically the Médoc and Graves). Cabernet Franc is also predominantly planted in the **Right Bank** (Saint-Émilion and Pomerol), and Malbec is most associated with the **Left Bank** (Médoc and Graves), where it was historically the dominant variety before phylloxera.
