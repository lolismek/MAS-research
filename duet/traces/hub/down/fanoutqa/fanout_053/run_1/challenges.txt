===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: Who are the founders of the remaining three largest companies by revenue in 2023?
ANSWER: The remaining three largest companies by revenue in 2023 are Apple, UnitedHealth Group, and Costco. Apple was founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne. UnitedHealth Group traces its origins to United Healthcare, which was founded by William W. Thompson. Costco was founded by James Sinegal and Jeffrey Brotman.

ANSWER: The five largest companies by revenue in 2023, based on the Fortune 500 ranking available in November 2023, are Walmart (founded by Sam Walton), Amazon (founded by Jeff Bezos), Apple (founded by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne), UnitedHealth Group (founded by William W. Thompson and Richard T. Burke), and Costco (founded by Jim Sinegal and Jeff Brotman).
