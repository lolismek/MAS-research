ANSWER: The highest elevations for the five largest states by land mass are: Alaska at 20,310 feet (Denali), Texas at 8,751 feet (Guadalupe Peak), California at 14,505 feet (Mount Whitney), Montana at 12,799 feet (Granite Peak), and New Mexico at 13,167 feet (Wheeler Peak).
