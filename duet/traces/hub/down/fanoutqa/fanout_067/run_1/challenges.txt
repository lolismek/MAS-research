===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: Please provide the remaining three richest people as of November 20, 2023, along with their high school and university graduation details.
ANSWER: As of November 20, 2023, the 3rd, 4th, and 5th richest people in the world were Jeff Bezos, Larry Ellison, and Warren Buffett. Jeff Bezos graduated from Miami Palmetto Senior High School and Princeton University. Larry Ellison graduated from Proviso East High School but did not complete his university studies, having attended the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign and the University of Chicago. Warren Buffett graduated from Washington High School in Omaha and Columbia University, where he earned his MBA.

===== ask 2 =====
QUESTION: Can you provide the names and educational backgrounds of all 5 richest people as of November 20, 2023?
ANSWER: As of November 20, 2023, the five richest people in the world were Elon Musk, Bernard Arnault, Jeff Bezos, Mark Zuckerberg, and Larry Ellison. ===== ask 3 =====
QUESTION: The report only covers the 4th richest person; can you please provide the educational backgrounds for the other four richest individuals (Bernard Arnault, Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos, and Warren Buffett) as of November 20, 2023?
ANSWER: Bernard Arnault graduated from École Polytechnique in France. Jeff Bezos graduated from Princeton University with a degree in electrical engineering and computer science. Warren Buffett attended the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, graduating in 1948, and later earned a master's degree from Columbia Business School.
