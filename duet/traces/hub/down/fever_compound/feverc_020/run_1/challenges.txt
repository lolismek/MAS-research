===== ask 2 =====
QUESTION: Did your research also verify the part of the claim that a polynomial consists of coefficients?
ANSWER: Yes, the claim that a polynomial consists of coefficients is correct. By mathematical definition, a polynomial is an expression composed of variables and coefficients, involving operations such as addition, subtraction, and multiplication. The coefficients are the constant numerical factors that multiply the variables (for example, in the expression $3x^2 + 2x - 5$, the numbers 3, 2, and -5 are the coefficients). Therefore, the presence of coefficients is a fundamental and necessary component of any polynomial.
