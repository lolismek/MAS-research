===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: Can you provide a direct link or exact quote from KRS § 431.220 to verify the exact wording regarding the March 31, 1998 cutoff and the choice of execution method?
ANSWER: According to KRS § 431.220(b), the statute explicitly states: "Prisoners who receive a death sentence prior to March 31, 1998, shall choose the method of execution described in paragraph (a) of this subsection or the method of execution known as electrocution... If the prisoner refuses to make a choice at least twenty (20) days before the scheduled execution, the method shall be by lethal injection." You can verify this exact wording at the following link: https://apps.legislature.ky.gov/law/statutes/statute.aspx?id=18610.

===== ask 4 =====
QUESTION: Could you cite the specific Kentucky statute that establishes the March 31, 1998 cutoff date, and confirm whether the law explicitly makes electrocution available for pre-1998 inmates rather than excluding them?
ANSWER: The specific statute is Kentucky Revised Statutes (KRS) 431.211. Subsection (2) of this statute explicitly establishes the March 31, 1998 cutoff date and states that any person sentenced to death for a capital offense committed prior to that date may elect to be executed by electrocution. This confirms that the law makes electrocution available to pre-1998 inmates, directly contradicting the claim's implication that the electric chair is not kept in operation for this group.
