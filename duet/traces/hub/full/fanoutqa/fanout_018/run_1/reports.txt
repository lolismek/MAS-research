===== edge 1 =====
I investigated the height of Mount Everest as of late November 2023. The current official height is 8,848.86 meters. This figure was established through a joint survey conducted by Nepal and China and officially announced on December 8, 2020. This measurement includes the snow cap and is the widely accepted standard used by both countries and international bodies like the National Geographic Society. No changes to this height have occurred between the 2020 announcement and the requested date in late 2023.

===== edge 2 =====
I investigated the heights of K2, Kangchenjunga, Lhotse, and Makalu as of late November 2023. Based on standard geographical data and recent surveys, the accepted heights are:
- K2: 8,611 meters
- Kangchenjunga: 8,586 meters
- Lhotse: 8,516 meters
- Makalu: 8,485 meters

For Makalu, I noted a historical discrepancy where older sources cited 8,463 meters, but modern surveys and current authoritative sources confirm 8,485 meters as the correct elevation. The other three peaks have stable, widely accepted measurements that have not changed recently. These figures represent the current state of the world as of the requested date.
