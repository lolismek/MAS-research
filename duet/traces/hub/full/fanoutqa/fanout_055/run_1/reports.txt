The assignment was to identify the longest river in Canada and its length in kilometers.

Based on the research, the longest river in Canada is the **Mackenzie River**. Its total length, including the Finlay-Peace-Mackenzie system, is **4,241 kilometers** (2,635 miles). While the main stem of the Mackenzie River is shorter (approximately 1,738 km), the system length is the standard measurement used by authoritative sources such as World Atlas, Wikipedia, and Britannica to designate it as the longest river in the country.

**Answer:** Mackenzie River, 4,241 km
