Based on the founding dates of German universities that have operated continuously without a significant closure, the five oldest are:
1. **Heidelberg University** (founded 1386)
2. **Leipzig University** (founded 1409)
3. **University of Rostock** (founded 1419)
4. **University of Greifswald** (founded 1456)
5. **University of Freiburg** (founded 1457)

*(Note: The University of Cologne, founded in 1388, is excluded because it was closed in 1798 and only reopened in 1919. LMU Munich and Tübingen were founded later, in 1472 and 1477, respectively.)*

As of November 20, 2023, the presidents (or rectors) of these five institutions were:
*   **Heidelberg University:** Jürgen Mlynek
*   **Leipzig University:** Timo Meixner
*   **University of Rostock:** Christiane Woopen
*   **University of Greifswald:** Christiane Joest-Hanke
*   **University of Freiburg:** Volker ter Meulen

FINAL ANSWER: Jürgen Mlynek, Timo Meixner, Christiane Woopen, Christiane Joest-Hanke, Volker ter Meulen
