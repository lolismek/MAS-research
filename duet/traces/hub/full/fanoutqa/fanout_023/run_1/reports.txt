I found that Cabernet Sauvignon is most predominantly planted in the **Left Bank** of Bordeaux. Specifically, it is most concentrated in the **Médoc** (including the Haut-Médoc) and **Graves** regions.

The Left Bank is characterized by gravelly soils, which are ideal for Cabernet Sauvignon, whereas the Right Bank (Saint-Émilion and Pomerol) is dominated by clay and limestone soils, making it the primary area for Merlot. The Médoc is widely considered the heartland of Cabernet Sauvignon in the region.
