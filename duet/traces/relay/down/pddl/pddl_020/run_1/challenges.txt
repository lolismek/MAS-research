===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: Is the rule actually "smaller disks on larger disks" as in standard Hanoi, because the stated goal requires placing larger disks on smaller ones?
ANSWER: No, the rule is actually the reverse of standard Hanoi: larger disks are placed on smaller disks. The observed size facts show that d1 is the largest and d4 is the smallest (e.g., `smaller(d2,d1)` means d2 < d1). Both the initial state and the goal state consist of larger disks stacked on top of smaller ones (e.g., `on(d1,d2)` places the larger d1 on the smaller d2). The valid actions also confirm this, allowing moves like `move(d1,d2)` where a larger disk is placed onto a smaller one.
