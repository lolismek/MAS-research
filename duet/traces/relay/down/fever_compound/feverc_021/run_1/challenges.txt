QUESTION: Can you provide the specific sources or links for the search results that confirm the 65-team field and single-elimination format for the 2003 tournament?
ANSWER: Based on the search results, the Britannica article (https://www.britannica.com/sports/March-Madness) confirms that the NCAA tournament is a "single-elimination" event and states that "A 65th team and corresponding 'play-in game' were added in 2001," remaining at that size until the field expanded to 68 teams in 2011. Additionally, an NCAA.com article (https://www.ncaa.com/news/basketball-men/article/2026-05-07/how-ncaa-mens-basketball-tournament-has-evolved-through-years) explicitly lists the tournament size as "65 teams" starting in 2001, which directly supports the claim for the 2003 tournament.
