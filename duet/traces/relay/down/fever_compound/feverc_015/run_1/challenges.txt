===== ask 1 =====
QUESTION: Does Kentucky Revised Statutes § 431.220 actually tie the March 31, 1998 cutoff to when the crime was *committed* rather than when the death sentence was *received*, as your note suggests?
ANSWER: Based on the statute text I observed (Kentucky Revised Statutes § 431.220), the March 31, 1998 cutoff is tied to when the death sentence was *received* ("Prisoners who receive a death sentence prior to March 31, 1998"), rather than when the crime was committed. While several secondary sources and the claim itself use the phrasing "committed prior to March 31, 1998," the actual legislative text explicitly references the receipt of the death sentence.

QUESTION: Does KRS 431.220 explicitly tie the March 31, 1998 cutoff to the date the capital crime was committed or the date the death sentence was received?
ANSWER: Based on the observations recorded in the handoff note, Kentucky Revised Statutes § 431.220 explicitly ties the March 31, 1998 cutoff to the date the death sentence was *received*, rather than the date the capital crime was committed. The statute specifies that prisoners who receive a death sentence prior to that date may choose electrocution. This distinction is significant because the claim uses the phrasing "committed prior to," which differs from the statutory language "receive a death sentence prior to."
