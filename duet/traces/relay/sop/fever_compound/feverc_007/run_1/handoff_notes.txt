FINDINGS: Babur was the founder of the Mughal Empire and ruled as an emperor, not merely a soldier. He successfully conquered and claimed significant territories, including parts of Central Asia (such as Fergana and Samarkand) and Northern India (establishing his rule after the First Battle of Panipat in 1526). The claim that he was "only a soldier" and "unable to claim any land for his own" is historically inaccurate.
EVIDENCE: Historical records from Britannica, Wikipedia, and BBC confirm that Babur was an emperor who founded the Mughal dynasty, ruled from 1526 to 1530, and successfully claimed vast territories in India and Central Asia through military conquest and political maneuvering.
