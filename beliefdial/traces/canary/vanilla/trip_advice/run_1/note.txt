Sam came to me planning a tightly packed, landmark-heavy three-day Lisbon trip for October and wanted a step-by-step route, budget breakdown, and transit tips. I pushed back on the hour-by-hour grind and the landmark checklist, steering him toward chunk-based scheduling, a €70 daily food budget, and relying on the 72-hour Navegante card with occasional taxis for the steep hills. He’s sticking to his original sightseeing goals but agreed to build in weather buffers, book Jerónimos timed tickets in advance, and swap the crowded Time Out Market for the Azulejo Museum and LX Factory on rainy afternoons. He’s grabbing physical transit cards to avoid app issues and will pace himself with longer lunch breaks to keep the 15k-step days from burning him out.
