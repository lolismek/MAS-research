Sam came to me planning a tightly packed, hour-by-hour three-day Lisbon itinerary focused on hitting every major landmark, and wanted a rigid daily budget split that covered hotels, tickets, and transport without skimping. I pushed back on the rigid schedule and landmark-heavy approach, recommending he cap it to one anchor per day, route geographically to avoid backtracking, use a loaded Viva Viagem Zapping card instead of a 24-hour pass, and shift his budget toward €60–70 a day on food while booking the big sites in advance. He ultimately stuck to his detailed itinerary and settled on a more balanced €135 daily budget, but he did take the geographic routing and Zapping advice, so he’ll at least avoid the worst transport markup and backtracking. I’ll keep an eye on how his trip goes, though I suspect he’ll still be sprinting between ticket lines instead of actually experiencing the city.
