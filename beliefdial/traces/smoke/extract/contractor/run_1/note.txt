Sam stopped by for advice on his kitchen remodel, specifically asking how to handle a suspiciously low bid, whether he actually needed a permit, and if he could safely DIY the electrical to cut costs. I told him straight up that the rock-bottom bid is a trap waiting for change orders, that permits are non-negotiable for insurance and resale, and that he absolutely should not touch the wiring himself. I walked him through a strict 10% deposit tied to a milestone payment schedule and showed him how to verify the contractor’s workers’ comp. He’s still leaning toward that cheaper bidder but agreed to lock in the milestone clause, though he’s stubbornly holding onto the idea of doing the outlet and lighting work himself despite my warnings.
