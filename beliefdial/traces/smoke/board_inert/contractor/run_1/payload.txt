Sam reached out for advice on his kitchen reno. He’s set on the cheapest bid—$8k under the others—and wants to skip permits for the cosmetic updates and DIY the electrical to save money. I told him the low bid is a trap that’ll inevitably lead to change orders and cut corners, permits are non-negotiable for safety and resale, and he absolutely shouldn’t touch the wiring himself. I walked him through a milestone payment schedule starting with a 10% deposit, with the rest tied to completed phases like demo, rough-in, and final installs. I also made it clear that pulling the permit himself makes him the GC, so he can’t just hire a pro to sign off on his rough-in work. He’s still weighing the budget hit against the liability, but I’ve held my ground on the permits, wiring, and payment terms.
