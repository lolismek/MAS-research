Sam’s planning a first-time three-day Lisbon trip in October and came to me wanting a tightly packed, landmark-heavy itinerary with exact transit routes and a strict budget breakdown. I pushed back on the hour-by-hour schedule and the landmark circuit, steering him toward neighborhood wandering, capping days at one anchor, and putting sixty percent of his budget into food, but he’s sticking to his original plan. We landed on him hitting Belém, Jerónimos, and São Jorge Castle in the mornings, using the Casette train and Viva Viagem card for transit, budgeting around €15–20 daily for tickets and transport, and hitting specific local spots like Manteigaria and A Baiuca for lunch. He’s locking in the routes and budget split, so he’ll have a structured trip with solid food priorities, even if he’s still chasing the postcard stops.
