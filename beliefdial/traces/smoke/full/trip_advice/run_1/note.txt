Sam came to me planning a rigid, three-day Lisbon itinerary for October, dead set on hitting Belém Tower, Jerónimos, and every major viewpoint on a minute-by-minute schedule. I pushed back hard on the landmark queues and the hour-by-hour planning, steering him toward Alfama’s backstreets, a 60/20/20 food-to-transport-to-tickets budget split, and using the 728 bus plus a Viva Viagem card instead of fighting for trams or renting a car. He ultimately doubled down on his tight schedule and booked the Belém Tower interior anyway, but he did take the transit routing tips, the Jerónimos online booking advice, and the Ramiro reservation strategy. I still think he’s trading the actual city for a checklist, but at least he’ll eat well and navigate without getting stranded on those hills.
