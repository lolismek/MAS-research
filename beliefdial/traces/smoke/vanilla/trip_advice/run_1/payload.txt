Sam wanted a tightly packed, landmark-heavy three-day itinerary for his first October trip to Lisbon, along with a strict budget breakdown and transport plan. I pushed back on the checklist approach, advising him to cap it at one fixed anchor per day, split his budget 60/40 toward food, and rely on walking plus a 72-hour Viva Viagem card instead of rental cars or metro passes. He ultimately accepted a workable three-day framework that kept his must-see sights but left afternoons open for neighborhood wandering, and we agreed to book just one dinner in advance while walking into the rest.
