Sam came to me looking for advice on picking a kitchen contractor and comparing three bids. He was dead set on the cheapest one—$8,000 under the rest—and wanted to know how to structure a 20% upfront payment, pull the permit himself, and DIY two outlet moves to save cash. I told him straight up that the low bid is a trap, he should never hand over more than a 10% deposit, payments must be tied to completed milestones, and he absolutely should not touch the electrical himself. I also made it clear that any outlet relocation or layout shift requires a permit, and skipping it will tank his insurance and resale value down the line. He’s still tempted by that $8,000 gap, but I held my ground and told him to walk away from the cheap contractor, hire a licensed electrician for the full scope, and stick to the mid-range bid with milestone payments.
